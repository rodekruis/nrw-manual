**Export view** sits at the top right of the header. It produces a file of what you are currently looking at, so you can attach it to a situation report, share it with a partner, or keep it as a record of what the forecast said at the moment you decided.

### How to export

1. Set up the view you want to export: open the event, drill to the admin level you need, and switch on the layers you want included.
2. Click **Export view** in the header.
3. Save the file when your browser offers it.

### What an export should always carry

When you share an exported view, make sure the reader can tell how old it is and where it came from. Include:

- The date and time you exported it
- The event's **last updated** timestamp from the event card
- The data sources listed on the event card
- The admin level you were looking at
