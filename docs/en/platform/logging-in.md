!!! Important "No login in this pre-release"
    The standalone platform is currently open without a login. Anyone with the link can open it and see the events for the country it is configured for. Accounts and sign in are planned for a later release.

### What this means for now

- You do not need credentials. Open the link you were given and the platform loads directly.
- There is no personal account, so there are no personal settings, saved views or per user preferences.
- Treat the link as shareable within your National Society and with the partners you would normally show a forecast to. If you are unsure whether something should be shared outside your organization, check with your focal point first.

### What is planned

Access to the standalone platform will run through its own entry point, separate from IFRC GO, so external partners such as government counterparts can be given access without needing a GO account. The National Risk Watch view embedded in IFRC GO uses GO accounts instead.
