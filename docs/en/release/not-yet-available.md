This is a pre-release. It is deliberately narrow. If you are coming from the IBF Portal, this page is the important one: it lists what you are used to that is not here yet.

### Not in this pre-release

| Not available | What this means for you | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hazards other than floods** | Only flood events appear. Drought and other hazards are not shown, even where you had them in IBF. | Planned. Drought is next in scope. |
| **Login and accounts** | The platform opens without credentials. No personal settings or saved views. See [Logging in](../platform/logging-in.md). | Planned. |
| **Alert emails and WhatsApp messages** | You will not be notified when an event opens or an alert level changes. You have to open the platform to see it. | Planned. |
| **Ended events** | Past events disappear from the overview instead of showing as ended. See [Event dates and statuses](../hazards/floods/dates-and-statuses.md). | In design. |
| **Export** | **Export view** is in the header but not yet functional. See [Exporting data](../platform/exporting.md). | Planned. |
| **Flood depth on low and medium events** | No flood map and no exposed population on events below the trigger return period. | Depends on source data availability. |
| **Total population** | Only exposed population is shown, so you cannot see it as a share of the people living in an area. | Planned, not prioritized for this release. |

!!! Warning "Do not rely on National Risk Watch alone for notification"
    Because alert emails and messages are not yet available, an event can open without anyone on your team noticing. Until notifications ship, keep whatever monitoring routine you already have in place, and check the platform on the cadence your protocol requires.

### What is in this pre-release

- Flood events for your country, with alert level, status, trigger indication and advisory
- Exposed population per admin area, with drill down to the lowest available level
- The flood depth layer on events that reach the trigger return period
- River discharge forecast graph with return period
- The data sources behind every event
- The standalone platform, usable on a laptop or on a large screen in an Emergency Operations Centre

### What we are asking of you

This release is for testing with real events and real decisions, not a finished product. What we need most is where it misleads you: a number you read differently than intended, a label that means something else in your context, a threshold that does not match your protocol. See [Limitations and feedback](../hazards/floods/limitations.md).
